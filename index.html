About the home

A well-kept 1998 colonial on a quiet dead-end street, with four bedrooms, two full baths and two half baths. Santos mahogany hardwood runs through the living and dining rooms and up to the second-floor landing.

The kitchen was fully remodeled in 2019, and the finished basement adds room to spread out. Out back, a private yard is built around an inground pool, ready for summer.

The big-ticket work is done: a new roof with 6-inch gutters, soffits and fascia in 2022, a newer water heater the same year, and a furnace and central air replaced in 2018.
